---
title: Use the bottom bar
summary: Switch between Chat, Apps, and Settings
---

The Wonton app has three bottom-bar entries:

- “聊天” (Chat): view chats, open rooms, and start a direct chat. When a confirmation card is waiting, a banner appears at the top; tap it to open the room.
- “应用” (Apps): view installed apps, open “应用商店” (App Store), check “连接” (Connection), add a device, or pair again.
- “设置” (Settings): change the app’s appearance and open “聊天记录备份” (Chat backup).

On the first launch, if the device is not paired, the app opens the “配对” (Pair) page inside Apps. After pairing, later launches return to Chat.

The bottom bar is hidden inside a chat room, app details, an install/update confirmation, an app web page, backup, or a Connection subpage. Use that page’s back control to return; the bar appears again. It also makes room while you type.

## When a confirmation is waiting

If a confirmation card is not in the room you are viewing, a banner appears above the bar, such as “标题，等你确认” (Title, waiting for your confirmation) or “2 张卡片等你确认” (2 cards waiting for your confirmation). Tap it to open the room. The Apps status area also shows “等确认” (Waiting for confirmation).
