---
title: My apps
summary: Open apps from the sidebar and arrange “我的应用”
---

The Wonton app’s sidebar has fixed rows for “聊天” (Chat), “商店” (Store), and “设置” (Settings), with “我的应用” (My apps) in between: the apps you added, in the order you arranged, each opening as a full page.

- Newly installed apps are appended to “我的应用” automatically; you can turn automatic adding off in Settings.
- Tap “编辑” (Edit) next to “我的应用” in the sidebar to arrange the list: reorder, remove, or add from “全部应用” (All apps).
- An app page opens as a full page, with a “···” and “关闭” (Close) capsule at the top right; tapping “关闭” returns to where the page was opened from. Inside the page, back first walks the web history (swipe from the left edge on iOS, the back button on Android), then closes the page.

On the first launch, if the device is not paired, the app opens the “配对” (Pair) page. After pairing, later launches return to Chat.

## When a confirmation is waiting

If a confirmation card is not in the room you are viewing, a banner appears at the top of the app, such as “标题，等你确认” (Title, waiting for your confirmation) or “2 张卡片等你确认” (2 cards waiting for your confirmation). Tap it to open the room. The Apps status area also shows “等确认” (Waiting for confirmation).
